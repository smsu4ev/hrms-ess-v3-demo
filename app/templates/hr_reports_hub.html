Reports & Exports

On-screen views plus Excel downloads. Every export is written to the audit trail with the exporting user, the report name and the filters used.

Master Data Export
Employee master list with filters; salary/bank columns only for viewers with salary permission.
 Additions & Deductions
Payroll register per period: components, additions, arrears, OT, gross, deductions, net.
 Absence Report
Working days, present, absent, late, absence rate %, optional Bradford factor.
 Leave Report
Leave ledger and leave-request transactions.
 Separation Report
Ex-employee register with tenure, type, reason, settlement status.
 Turnover Report
Separations vs headcount; voluntary/involuntary split; per-department.
 OT Summary
Overtime hours and amounts by tier per payroll period.
 Payroll Variance
Current vs previous period: change, joiner/leaver buckets, 10% exceptions.
 Leave & EOSB Liability
Accrued gratuity and annual-leave value per employee.
 WPS Pre-Flight Check
Data-quality flags before bank transfer: IDs, IBAN, bank, pay limits.
 Loan Balances
Loans and advances: principal, recovered, balance, installment, status.

Scheduled (automatic) report delivery is not included - see README-V3.1 for the roadmap.
